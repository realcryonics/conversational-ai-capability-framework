# Limitations and publication checklist

## Scientific limitations

- The seven levels and eight dimensions are a **proposed design**, not a validated psychological hierarchy.
- Stars are subjective capability requirement hypotheses, not measurements; no calibration or inter-rater reliability has been established.
- The source's intervention-depth percentage bands lack an operational definition.
- Higher LV is not necessarily harder on every capability (the profiles are non-nested).
- The framework may be incomplete, overlapping or overly granular.
- Whether it predicts response quality better than simpler taxonomies is an empirical question.

## Rights and confidentiality before public release

- Confirm personal vs employment-related IP ownership for the framework and specific wording.
- Obtain necessary permission for any work-created material if required.
- Do not publish the source comparison workbook, character replies from EVE/Aven or Ethan, proprietary prompts, internal model versions, product metrics, or real-user data.
- If cleared, add an appropriate license with verified copyright holder(s).
- If any content remains disputed, keep the repository private and use only independently owned work for a public version.
