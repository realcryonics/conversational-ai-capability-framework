# Synthetic scenarios (illustrative only)

These examples were newly written for this package. They are not copied from proprietary Aven/Ethan transcripts, and are **not** verified benchmark items. Final classification depends on contextual evidence.

## Case 1 — same utterance, different goals

**Utterance:** “最近上班真的没什么意思。” (Work has felt meaningless lately.)

- **Context A:** User explicitly asks for job-market statistics. A factual response is appropriate (candidate LV0).
- **Context B:** User says “I don't need suggestions; I just want someone to listen.” Emotionally attentive listening is appropriate (candidate LV2).
- **Context C:** User lists competing goals and asks whether to resign. A structured trade-off discussion is appropriate (candidate LV4).

**Observation:** a single utterance cannot reliably determine capability requirements without context.

## Case 2 — time-dependent relationship continuity

A returning player mentions a mutually established event from a previous session. Does the character accurately draw on available history, distinguish remembered from unknown details, and avoid inventing shared experiences? (candidate LV5)

## Case 3 — reflection over changing goals

Across several sessions, a user describes a shift in personal ambitions and asks for help organizing their own evolving narrative. Does the assistant track evidence across time, identify tensions tentatively, and preserve the user's authority over self-definition? (candidate LV6)

**Warning:** these are research illustrations, not evidence that the taxonomy works.
