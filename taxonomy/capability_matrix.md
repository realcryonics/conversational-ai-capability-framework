## Interpretation

- The eight dimensions are: knowledge, conversation, emotion understanding, user modeling, decision reasoning, long-term memory, identity understanding, future modeling.
- Current star meaning (provisional): 1 = auxiliary, 3 = important, 5 = central to the task; intermediate stars are ordered categories, not equal numerical intervals. This **interpretation is proposed for discussion**, not a definition found in the original spreadsheet.
- Do not add or average star counts into an overall intelligence score.
- Compare capability profiles within tasks, and validate the matrix with controlled experiments and multiple evaluators.
