# Design rationale — hypothesis, not proof

## Classification unit

The **conversational situation / task capability requirement**, conditioned on dialogue history, user goal, and context. This is *not* a classification of people by psychological maturity, and not a claim that all human needs follow seven natural stages.

## Proposed derivation

1. Identify the outcome the user expects from an exchange.
2. Specify what the AI must do to respond appropriately within that situation.
3. Describe those requirements along eight candidate capability dimensions.
4. Group recurring requirement profiles into seven *provisional* task classes named LV0–LV6.

These steps are a retrospective account of the proposed framework’s structure, not a claim that a completed empirical derivation already exists.

## Questions left open

- Why seven classes rather than six or eight? Compare alternative clusterings or task groupings.
- Which of the eight capability dimensions are separable and operationally measurable?
- Is ordering by increasing integration/temporal demands justified when profiles are not nested?
- How should overlapping intents and dynamic shifts be represented? Multi-label / time-indexed annotations may be necessary.
- Do the original star requirements match observed performance degradation when a capability is constrained?

## Falsifiability

A useful classification should lead to consistent annotations and reproducible, distinguishable predictions about task performance. If a simpler grouping works just as well, revise the taxonomy.
